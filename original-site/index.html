Who we are

Faith that moves a city.

CTPMI is a non-denominational church built on prayer — easily accessible from Overport and the surrounding suburbs, and always ready to welcome you home. We believe every testimony, every life lesson and every godly passion is a message God wants told, and we exist to preach the gospel, pray for the sick, and stand against every work of darkness in this generation.

"Jesus Christ is Lord."

Cell ministry

160+
prayer cells meeting across Durban — and counting.

Small groups, citywide impact.

Every cell is shepherded by leadership committed to consistency — because we don't drop what God has trusted us to carry.

Zone Pastors
Oversee clusters of cells across each region of the city, discipling leaders and covering the harvest in prayer.
District Leaders
Coordinate cell leaders within a district, keeping momentum, order and stability in the ministry.
Cell Leaders
Open their homes and hearts weekly — teaching the word, praying for needs, and winning souls one household at a time.
